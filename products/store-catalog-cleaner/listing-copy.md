# Store Catalog Cleaner — draft storefront copy

**Status:** private draft; not published or commercially validated.

## Suggested title

Store Catalog Cleaner — Offline CSV Product & Inventory Cleanup Tool

## Short description

Clean up product and inventory CSV files before importing them into your store. Check missing values, blank rows, duplicate records, and messy column names, then export a cleaned copy. Runs locally in your browser; your CSV is not uploaded.

## What's included

- One standalone index.html browser app
- Offline use; no login or subscription
- CSV import, preview, cleanup, and export
- Built-in sample file
- Setup notes and limitations

## Intended customer

Small online-store operators who periodically receive or maintain product/inventory data in CSV format.

## Price experiment

No price is validated. If a seller chooses to test the product, begin with a low one-time price (for example, USD 5) and measure real visits, purchases, refunds, support requests, and net proceeds. Do not describe this as a proven market price.

## Claims we can support

- Runs in a modern browser without an installation.
- Processes the selected CSV locally in the browser.
- Does not send file contents to a remote service.
- Supports CSV, not Excel workbook files.
- Offers generic data cleanup; it is not certified for a specific ecommerce platform.

## Do not claim

- Guaranteed sales, conversion, rankings, or revenue improvement.
- Compatibility with every store-import schema.
- Automatic tax, accounting, or legal compliance.
- Support for .xlsx files.
- That the product has customers, reviews, or sales.
