## Full description

Want a clearer Etsy listing that better explains your digital product and uses relevant search phrases?

I will review one public Etsy listing and compare it with up to five relevant listings in the same niche. You will receive:

- A structured review of title, description, tags, product clarity and differentiation.
- Up to ten keyword/phrase ideas, each linked to the public source or search observation used.
- A revised, readable title and description draft.
- Up to 13 varied, relevant tag suggestions checked against Etsy's current 20-character tag limit.
- Five prioritized actions you can apply yourself.
- A PDF report and editable worksheet.

**Research standard:** I distinguish public observations from recommendations. I do not invent search-volume numbers or claim access to Etsy's private shop analytics. Free public search observations and Google Trends (when relevant) are labelled as qualitative evidence, not exact Etsy search-volume data.

**Important:** Etsy search considers more than keywords, including listing and shop factors. I cannot guarantee ranking, traffic, sales or conversion improvements. You remain responsible for checking product facts, rights, category/attributes and final changes before publishing.

No shop password or account access is required.

## Project steps

## FAQs

**Do you need access to my Etsy account?**
No. A public listing URL and accurate product facts are enough for this package.

**Do you provide exact keyword search volumes?**
No. The starter package uses sourced public observations and qualitative keyword hypotheses. I do not invent volume estimates or represent Google Trends as Etsy search volume.

**Can you guarantee better ranking or sales?**
No. Etsy search uses multiple factors and outcomes depend on the listing, product, shop and competition. I provide researched recommendations, not guaranteed outcomes.

**Will you publish the changes for me?**
No. This package provides drafts and recommendations. You retain control of your shop and final publication.

**What if my listing is not in English?**
The starter package is limited to one supported language that can be reliably researched and reviewed. Confirm language availability before ordering.
