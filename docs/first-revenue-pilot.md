# First-Revenue Pilot: Evidence-Based Etsy Listing Audit

Status: internal commercial experiment — draft only. Not published, not an offer to any customer, and not evidence of revenue.

## Objective

Earn the economy's first legitimate customer payment by selling one bounded, repeatable research service. The first milestone is one completed order and a verified net receipt, not more infrastructure features.

## Why this offer

Public Fiverr listings show buyers reviewing and purchasing Etsy SEO, keyword research, listing optimization and shop-audit services. Examples include:
- A Pakistan-based Etsy SEO listing with 169 reviews: https://www.fiverr.com/uzma_seo007/boost-sales-by-doing-seo-of-your-etsy-store-listings
- A Pakistan-based product-research listing with 10 reviews and a public description of keyword, competitor and listing deliverables: https://www.fiverr.com/amirshahzad941/do-etsy-product-research-hunting-for-pl-and-seo-listings
- A Pakistan-based Etsy listing optimization provider with public review history and example project prices: https://www.fiverr.com/yourbestext/do-etay-title-and-13-tag-to-increase-your-search-results

These are evidence that this category has paying marketplace activity, not proof of demand for this new account, guaranteed conversion, or verified sales volume.

Etsy's official guidance confirms that listings can use up to 13 tags, each up to 20 characters, and that search considers multiple listing and shop factors. It does not promise that keyword changes guarantee ranking or sales:
- https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/382774281517
- https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000336307-How-to-Use-Tags-to-Get-Found-in-Search

## Proposed offer

**Working title:** I will audit one Etsy digital product listing and deliver an evidence-backed SEO improvement plan.

**Ideal buyer:** An Etsy seller with an existing digital-download listing who wants practical listing improvements without granting shop access.

**Introductory test price:** USD 15 for one listing, subject to the marketplace's displayed fee and final owner approval. This is a price experiment, not a researched market average.

**Delivery target:** 3 calendar days after all required information is received.

### Included

1. Audit one public Etsy listing URL.
2. Review up to five relevant public competitor listings in the same product niche and target market.
3. Record up to ten buyer-intent keyword/phrase hypotheses, with the exact source and observation for each. These are qualitative candidates, not paid-tool search-volume estimates.
4. Draft one clearer title and one revised listing description.
5. Suggest up to 13 varied, relevant tags; validate each against Etsy's 20-character tag limit and avoid unsupported claims.
6. Provide five prioritized, actionable improvements covering listing clarity, keyword relevance, differentiation and buyer information.
7. Deliver a concise PDF report plus an editable CSV/XLSX keyword and competitor worksheet.

### Not included

- No guaranteed ranking, traffic, sales, revenue, or conversion lift.
- No paid keyword-tool metrics or invented search volumes.
- No shop login, password, API key, buyer data, or account access.
- No listing upload, shop edits, ads, purchases, or external account actions.
- No copying competitor text, images, trademarks, or protected designs.
- No legal, tax, trademark, or platform-policy certification.
- No automated scraping or bypassing platform restrictions.

## Research and production method

Use public pages and free tools only:
- Etsy search suggestions and public search results, recorded with date, query and target market.
- Public competitor listing pages, with URLs and observable fields only.
- Google Trends where relevant, clearly labelled as relative interest rather than absolute Etsy search volume.
- Etsy Seller Handbook and Help Center for platform constraints.
- A structured report template and mechanical tag-length/duplicate checks.

Every factual claim in the report must be linked to its source. Distinguish observed facts, reasoned interpretations and recommendations. If a source cannot be checked, mark it unknown rather than filling the gap.

## Delivery quality gate

Do not deliver until:
- All competitor URLs open and match the buyer's product niche.
- The report covers exactly the agreed listing and target market.
- Every keyword hypothesis has a source and a confidence label.
- No search volume, ranking, sales or conversion outcome is fabricated or guaranteed.
- All suggested tags are no more than 20 characters and are not exact duplicates.
- Draft copy accurately describes the buyer's actual product and makes no unsupported product claims.
- The final report and worksheet agree with each other.
- A second review catches factual, spelling, formatting and policy issues.

## First-sales experiment

1. Owner checks Upwork account eligibility, identity status and withdrawal methods.
2. Owner reviews the exact project description, scope, price, delivery time and samples.
3. Owner explicitly approves publication. No listing is published by this document or by the local economy prototype.
4. Keep the offer visible and monitor impressions, clicks, enquiries, orders, cancellations and completed net receipts weekly.
5. If there are impressions but no clicks, revise the title/gallery; if clicks but no orders, revise offer clarity, sample and price; if no impressions, revise category/search tags and do not assume demand.
6. After the first paid order, fulfil under owner supervision, confirm acceptance and payment clearance, deduct platform and withdrawal fees, then record only the net amount actually received.

## Owner approval gates

The following require separate explicit approval:
- Creating or modifying the freelancer account.
- Completing identity or tax verification.
- Publishing or changing the public service listing.
- Accepting any order or customer commitment.
- Delivering customer-specific work.
- Any purchase, paid tool, paid subscription, ad spend, refund, withdrawal or transfer.

No customer payment is routed through the autonomous economy prototype. The first order must use the marketplace's own permitted payment and dispute process. Do not accept off-platform payment to bypass fees or rules.

## Definition of first revenue

First revenue is verified only when:
1. A real buyer places and pays for an order through an approved channel.
2. The agreed work is delivered and accepted under the platform's rules.
3. Funds clear and are withdrawn to an owner-controlled, matching-name account.
4. The net amount after marketplace and withdrawal fees is reconciled and recorded.

A listing, click, enquiry, order awaiting clearance, simulated ledger entry or platform balance that cannot yet be withdraw is not verified net revenue.
