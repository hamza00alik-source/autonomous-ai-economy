# Agent Protocol

Every task must be explicit and independently verifiable.

## Required task fields

- Unique task ID
- Objective and business rationale
- Assigned agent and required capabilities
- Input references and permitted data sources
- Expected deliverable and acceptance criteria
- Deadline or execution budget, if applicable
- Risk classification: low, medium, or high
- Approval requirement and approving owner
- Evidence references and final verification status

## Collaboration rules

1. The controller assigns one accountable lead agent.
2. Specialist agents may produce subtask proposals, but cannot silently expand scope.
3. Agents communicate through structured task records, not hidden shared assumptions.
4. Each handoff includes output, evidence, known limitations, and next requested action.
5. A verifier distinct from the producer checks acceptance criteria for material deliverables.
6. Unverified work cannot be marked complete or used to trigger a financial action.

## Permission boundaries

Agents may research and draft within their assigned scope. Publishing, sending binding offers, signing contracts, spending money, changing access controls, and transferring real funds require explicit owner approval until a future policy is reviewed and authorized.
