# Brand Naming Research

## Brand direction

The public product name should be a distinctive, pronounceable coined word that can represent an open economy for human and AI participants, independent agents, services, verified work, and self-funding compute. Avoid names that over-promise autonomy, imply guaranteed income, or sound like a crypto token.

The GitHub repository slug `autonomous-ai-economy` may remain unchanged. The product brand, legal entity name, domain and repository slug are separate decisions.

## Preliminary collision screen

A quick public-web screen on 17 November 2026 found clear existing uses for several earlier ideas:

- **Synthora** — already used by multiple AI/software businesses, including Synthora AI Labs and registered company listings. Reject.
- **Autonex** — used by multiple technology and AI businesses, including Autonex AI and Autonex software/industrial products. Reject.
- **Econova** — used by multiple technology/AI businesses and other companies. Reject.
- **Auronex** — existing software/AI company and product usage. Reject.
- **Ecoryn** — no obvious exact-match result surfaced in the limited search performed. This is only an unverified lead, not a finding of availability or legal clearance.

Sources: public search results for Synthora, Autonex, Econova and Auronex; see research notes in the project conversation. This preliminary screen is not a complete global company, domain, app-store, social-handle, GitHub or trademark search.

## Working shortlist and expanded collision screen

The following coined candidates were also checked with exact-match web searches and rejected as already used or too close to existing software/AI businesses:

- **Veylora** — existing AI ecosystem and AI/blockchain/fintech software businesses.
- **Orvanta** — existing AI orchestration and business-solutions brands.
- **Aevoryn** — existing technology company usage.
- **Aevaryn** — existing technology company and product portfolio.
- **Nexoryn** — multiple active software/technology businesses, including a Pakistan-based company.
- **Orynex** — existing software/product engineering business.
- **Kynora** — existing AI software/app usage.

**ECORYN** remains the only current provisional lead from this limited screen because no obvious exact-match result surfaced in the searches performed. This is not proof of availability. Before recommending it as a final choice, test its pronunciation, spelling, meanings and phonetic variants, then check company registries, domains, social handles and trademark databases.

Next naming pass should generate fresh candidates from less crowded linguistic roots rather than repeatedly modifying common AI/eco/nexus/ora/oryn suffixes. Keep a candidate only if it has no material same-sector collision, confusingly similar spelling/pronunciation, undesirable cross-language meaning, or obvious domain/handle conflict.

No candidate is approved as the final product name yet.

## Required clearance before adoption

- Search exact and phonetic variants across general web, GitHub, package registries, app stores and major social platforms.
- Check relevant domains through a registrar/WHOIS/RDAP lookup.
- Search trademark databases in target jurisdictions, including Pakistan and intended commercial markets; review relevant classes.
- Check company registries and local naming restrictions.
- Obtain qualified trademark advice before commercial launch.
- Reserve the chosen domain and handles only after explicit owner approval and cost review.

A search engine returning no exact result does not prove uniqueness, domain availability, or trademark clearance.
